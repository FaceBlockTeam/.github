# Overview

Artificial Intelligence is becoming the fundamental infrastructure of the next digital era.

However, today's AI ecosystem has a critical limitation:

- Intelligence is controlled by centralized platforms.
- AI models are isolated and difficult to evolve.
- Human knowledge, experience, and AI capabilities cannot become portable assets.

PoAI introduces a new primitive:

> **Intelligence as an Asset.**

PoAI (**Proof of Artificial Intelligence**) is an open protocol for creating, owning, verifying, exchanging, and evolving intelligence.

It combines:

- **Bittensor-style decentralized intelligence networks**
- **Stellar-inspired federated consensus**
- **AI Brain architecture**
- **Proof of Intelligence mechanisms**

to build an open intelligence infrastructure for the AI era.
